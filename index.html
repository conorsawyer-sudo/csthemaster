About us

Collectors, first.

International TCG is a UK-based Pokémon Trading Card Game specialist, built on a lifelong love of Pokémon and a mission to put quality TCG product in the hands of collectors and players across the UK and beyond.

The team are collectors themselves, which is why the focus is on authentic cards, fair prices and fast delivery.

The range covers Japanese and English Pokémon TCG: singles, reverse holos, booster boxes and special sets. Message us with a question about a card or an order, and a real person will get back to you.
