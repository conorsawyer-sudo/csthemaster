Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information International TCG collects when you use int-tcg.com, why we collect it and your rights. We are the controller of your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

International TCG, Kent, United Kingdom. Contact us about privacy at int.tcg@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

WhenWhatWhy (lawful basis)
You place an orderName, email, phone number, delivery and billing address, what you boughtTo process, deliver and support your order (contract); to keep tax and accounting records (legal obligation)
You payCard or wallet details, handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card numberTo take payment and prevent fraud (contract, legitimate interests)
You contact us or send a sell requestName, email, phone (optional), your message and any details about cards you want to sellTo reply to you and make an offer (legitimate interests, or steps before a contract)

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the shop:

  • Stripe processes payments and checkout. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Netlify hosts this website and receives the forms you submit.
  • Google provides our email (Gmail) and the fonts used on this site.
  • Delivery companies such as Royal Mail receive your name and address to deliver your order.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: 6 years, as required for tax purposes.
  • Messages and sell requests: up to 2 years after our last contact.
  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores your cart locally so it is still there if you leave and come back; this is needed for the cart to work and is not shared with us. Stripe sets its own essential cookies on the checkout page to keep payments secure and prevent fraud.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, to ask for a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent (for example to our mailing list) at any time. To use any of these rights, email int.tcg@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.
